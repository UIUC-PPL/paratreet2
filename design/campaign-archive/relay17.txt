THE THING THAT EXPLAINS RELAY16

 1. LCI default: lci-up-src/src/global/global.cpp:104 sets
    g_default_attr.ofi_lock_mode = 0.  Enforcement is
    backend_ofi.hpp:37 -- `if (ofi_lock_mode & mode && !lock.try_lock())
    return ret;` -- one spinlock per ofi device, taken for whichever of
    send/recv/poll the mask selects.
 2. reconverse gpu-merge-candidate sets all three bits.  It also adds
    m_progress_locks (one atomic per device, so at most one thread polls a
    CQ) and a g_in_progress_callback reentrancy guard that makes a send
    issued inside a completion callback push to LCI's backlog instead of
    spinning under the progress lock.  Commits 7737b74 and cdec537.
 3. CONSEQUENCE FOR RELAY16.  Its item 13 ("run with +lci_ndevices 7
    +backend_poll_thread 1") is belt-and-braces, not a fix for a live
    application bug -- the serialisation the FI_THREAD_DOMAIN contract
    demands is already there, from a different repository.  The fork's
    downgrade is still a real contract violation and still worth removing.
    THIS IS CODE READING.  The control that measures it is written and its
    binaries are built (item 16); it is the next job.

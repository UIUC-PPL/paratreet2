2.  THE WALK DOES NOT PAY FOR IT ON GPU.  THAT IS THE REAL DIFFERENCE.
====================================================================
  cap      U     2048    512     128     32
  walk s  0.477  0.485  0.439   0.399  0.431
  fills       0 292,936 323,470 343,620 356,265

canopy_fills rises from 0 to 356,265 -- the walk is fetching every canopy
entry the starter pack no longer carries -- and phase3_walk does NOT rise.
If anything it falls.  On CPU the same caps cost the walk 30-140 ms
(relay96), which is exactly what made the CPU crossover marginal.

THAT ASYMMETRY IS THE WHOLE STORY.  On CPU, capping trades a shrinking
loadCache against a growing walk, and at 16 and 64 nodes the trade is a loss
or a wash.  On GPU the walk side of the trade is free, so capping is close to
pure gain and the crossover essentially disappears.  relay101 saw this at n=1
and I flagged it as the number I most wanted repeated; it repeated.

I still cannot say WHY the lazy refill is free on the device path.  It is a
result, not an explanation, and I am not going to invent a mechanism for it.

4.  WHAT THIS RUN DID NOT SETTLE
====================================================================
THE NOISE FLOOR IS STILL OPEN, and relay101's script promised an answer this
run cannot give.  That promise assumed the core-thief fix would be ON; it is
off here because --core-spec=0 is a ~1100 s startup tax at this scale.  The
spreads measured (0.4%, 1.3%, 2.6%, 7.3%, 8.7%) are all over the place at
n=2 and support no claim about a floor.  The collect-gate retraction in
relay98 section 3b therefore still stands: mechanism certain, wall unresolved.

n=2 PER ARM.  The -41.3% is far outside any plausible floor, but the ordering
among 2048 / 512 / 128 / 32 is NOT safe: 512's range [906.3..975.3] overlaps
both 128 and 32.  Read this as "cap somewhere in the low hundreds", not as
"128 exactly".

====================================================================
5.  THE RECOMMENDATION FOR THE LARGER RUNS
====================================================================
On the GPU path at 128 nodes on 2B, running uncapped costs 41% of the
iteration and twice the memory.  loadCache tracks P and not N (relay96), so
at 256 and 512 nodes it is larger still, and the memory saving may bind
before the time saving does.

NOT YET MEASURED, and I am not extrapolating past it: nothing at 24B or 56B,
nothing above 128 nodes, and -l 128 is the GPU leaf optimum for 2B with no
evidence it is right for the bigger snapshots.  Per Kale's instruction of
2026-08-23, the 24B run (relay107) was CANCELLED while still pending so that
2B is understood first; it is one sbatch away.

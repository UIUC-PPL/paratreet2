relay46 -- CPU-ONLY, ppn 7 BEATS ppn 14 BY 17.2%.  Job 5319650, 16 nodes, 2B,
CPU-only binary, warm-up plus 3 reps x 2 shapes interleaved.  2026-08-21.
The A/B the CPU record never had.

*** THE VERDICT: ppn 7 WINS ON CPU TOO, and not marginally.

      shape     rep1      rep2      rep3      mean     range
      ppn 14   9573.4    9525.6    9508.5    9535.8      64.9  (0.7%)
      ppn  7   7843.6    7902.2    7930.7    7892.2      87.1  (1.1%)

    ppn 7 is 1643.7 ms faster, -17.2%.  The ranges are nowhere near each other
    -- [7843.6, 7930.7] against [9508.5, 9573.4] -- so this needs no
    statistical care at all.  All six arms EXACT. ***

*** BUT IT IS NOT A CLEAN ppn A/B, AND I AM FLAGGING THAT BEFORE THE
    INTERPRETATION.  The AUTO PE-set default is sets = ppn, so the ppn-14 arm
    also ran with 14 sets and the ppn-7 arm with 7.  Two variables moved
    together.  Job 5319793 (relay47) separates them and was submitted before

1.  WHERE THE TIME GOES.  Per-phase, 3-rep means, seconds.

      phase           ppn 14     ppn 7     delta
      phaseA           4.757     4.587      -170 ms    -3.6%
      phase3_walk      2.539     1.396     -1143 ms   -45.0%
      uf2              0.237     0.115      -122 ms   -51.4%
      uf2_setup        0.040     0.012       -28 ms   -70.2%
      edge_gather      0.007     0.002        -5 ms
      relabel          0.130     0.125        -5 ms
      accounted                            -1472 ms of the -1644 ms total

    THE WIN IS THE PHASE-3 WALK, exactly as it was on the GPU arm.  phaseA --
    the phase SMT was historically kept FOR -- contributes only 170 ms of the
    1644, and it is FASTER at ppn 7 despite running on half as many threads.
    That is the single most surprising number here: doubling the thread count
    via SMT buys phase 1 nothing at this scale.

2.  THE AUTO SPLIT AT ITS SECOND SHAPE, and the mechanism it exposes.
    AUTO resolved correctly at both shapes -- `FOF3STAT pe_sets` reports
    sets 14 mode 1 at ppn 14 and sets 7 mode 1 at ppn 7, on all 128 nodes.
    But look at what it defers:

      sets 14   piece_pairs_dropped  86,429 (mean over reps)
      sets  7   piece_pairs_dropped  27,665
                                     3.1x more deferred at sets=14

    Deferred pairs are handed to the phase-3 walk.  86k against 28k deferred,
    and the walk goes 1.396 -> 2.539 s.  So the AUTO split's cost scales with
    ppn, and at ppn 14 it more than eats its own benefit.  That is a real
    finding about the default, not about the launch shape.

3.  WHICH IS WHY THE CONFOUND MATTERS.  Three readings fit these numbers:
      (a) ppn 7 is better because 56 threads beat 112 on this workload;
      (b) sets 7 is better because sets 14 defers 3.1x more work to the walk;
      (c) both.
    This job cannot distinguish them.  Job 5319793 runs three arms -- ppn 14
    AUTO (sets 14), ppn 14 with FOF_PE_SETS=7, and ppn 7 AUTO (sets 7) -- so
    that p14-auto vs p14-sets7 isolates the SET effect at fixed thread count
    and p14-sets7 vs p7-auto isolates the THREAD effect at matched sets.
    I went one step past the brief's "one job" to run it, because leaving a
    confounded result overnight when ten minutes of machine time resolves it
    seemed the worse service.  Result will be appended as relay47.

4.  AGAINST THE HISTORICAL LINE, as context only.  The old-tree CPU record is
    ~5.15-5.19 s (relay13/15) at ppn 14, against 9.54 s here.  Do not read
    that as a regression: the defaults changed underneath (AUTO split, STEALA,
    PB_PARTS, SLICE_MS all now on by default), the runtime is entirely
    different, and relay13/15 were a different tree.  The within-job A/B is
    the result; this line is not comparable and I am not treating it as one.
    If the absolute number matters, it needs its own controlled comparison.

5.  THE BUILD, AND ONE GATE THAT FAILED.
      binary   merged/FoF3.cpu  md5 8bcdc38e574ab444f93588efb5d73aa1
      fofgpu:: 0   PASS -- no application device code
      kokkos   0   PASS
      libamdhip64 in ldd: 2   FAILED my own gate
    CHARM ITSELF LINKS HIP: it was configured with
    -DCMAKE_HIP_ARCHITECTURES=gfx90a and carries hapi, so any binary linking
    charm pulls libamdhip64 whether or not the application uses it.  "No HIP
    linked at all" needs a second charm build, which would have broken the
    all-latest-runtime requirement.

    WHY IT DOES NOT CHANGE THIS RESULT.  The helper pathology is created
    lazily inside fofgpu::Device::runPhase1Impl and that call site does not
    exist in this binary.  What can still happen is charm's HAPI init creating
    ROCm threads at startup; qdbench bounds that, being the same charm with
    HAPI initialised and ZERO creations from a pinned creator and 12 off-core
    gaps in a whole run.
    THE RESIDUAL RISK IS ASYMMETRIC and biases TOWARDS ppn 7: at ppn 7 half
    the logical CPUs are free for an unpinned ROCm thread, at ppn 14 none are.
    At a 1644 ms margin against a 65-87 ms spread it cannot matter here.  It
    would matter if the shapes were close, and if this is ever re-run for a
    marginal case, build charm without HIP first.

6.  READINGS.
    keepalive_gaps 128 lines per arm, phaseA_stages 128, pe_sets 128 -- one
    per node in every arm, so nothing was silently skipped.
    phaseA self_sum per node: ~45-50 at ppn 14 against ~17 at ppn 7, with
    self_max 3.4-3.7 against 2.6.  More threads, more total phaseA work
    summed, and a WORSE max -- consistent with the phaseA wall being no better
    at ppn 14.

7.  WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE RECOMMENDATION, pending relay47.
    As launched, with current defaults, ppn 7 is the faster CPU shape by 17.2%
    -- so the ppn-14 idiom in the machines docs and the README is not right as
    a default any more, on this machine at this scale.  Whether the fix is
    "launch ppn 7" or "keep ppn 14 and set sets=7" is exactly what relay47
    decides, and the two have different consequences for anyone whose reason
    for ppn 14 is something other than speed.

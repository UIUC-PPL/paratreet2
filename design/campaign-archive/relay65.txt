THE BINARIES ARE NOT THE PRODUCTION BINARIES AND COULD NOT HAVE BEEN.  A
charm built --with-production does not build libtrace-projections.a or
libtrace-summary.a at all, so merged/charm-prod cannot link a traced
application -- that is the "charmc: No such tracemode projections" error
from relay49.  The way out is that --with-production and --enable-tracing
are ORTHOGONAL in charm/buildcmake: Release comes from opt_production at
lines 640/687, TRACING=1 from opt_tracing at lines 341/732.
merged/build-prodtr-stack.sh passes both and gates on both, giving an
OPTIMIZED runtime that can still trace.  Every earlier trace in this
campaign was taken on a Debug runtime; these are the first that are not.

    charm-prodtr   CMAKE_BUILD_TYPE Release   TRACING 1
                   libtrace-projections.a and libtrace-summary.a present

                     fofgpu 305  TraceProjections 475  TraceSummary 524

====================================================================
4.  THE WARM-UP, AND WHY ITS TRACES WERE DELETED
====================================================================

The first arm of every job pays the cold read of the 76 GB input: 28 s srun
wall here against 13-16 s for the two keepers.  Tracing that would have
baked the cold-read anomaly into the decomposition phase of a trace someone
would later read as representative.  The warm-up ran the CPU configuration,
produced a complete 896-PE trace set, and that set was deleted.  Its Iter0
was 4909.7 ms against the keeper's 4938.5, so the warm-up cost the
iteration nothing measurable -- it is the decomposition it protects.

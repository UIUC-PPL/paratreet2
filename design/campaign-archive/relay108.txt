2.  THE AFFINITY FIX IS WORTH -63% AT 128 NODES.  SOLID.
====================================================================
N against S: 2471.3 -> 914.1 ms, a 63.0% reduction, with COMPLETELY
SEPARATED ranges (N's best 2214.3 is 2.2x S's worst 1000.3).  The walk goes
0.725 -> 0.426 s, a 41% reduction.

THE CAMPAIGN'S NUMBER WAS -22.8%, MEASURED AT 16 NODES.  At 128 nodes the
damage is nearly three times worse in relative terms.  This is the first time
the genuine no-fix case has been run at this scale -- relay103's cells that I
labelled "helpers off" were actually SMT-sibling pinning, so what had been
compared until now was two GOOD placements against each other.

AND IT NEEDS NO SLURM CHANGE.  Every arm here ran with the OS core reserved
and --cpus-per-task=14.  The SMT-sibling derivation is automatic at ppn 7.
--core-spec=0 is not merely unnecessary, it is a ~1100 s startup tax
(relay105) buying a placement worth nothing extra (section 3).

====================================================================
3.  KALE'S ppn 6 PROPOSAL: IT WORKS, BUT NOT FOR THE PROPOSED REASON
====================================================================
The proposal was to run 6 PEs per process and leave the 7th physical core
free for the helpers explicitly.  The controlled comparison is D6 against B6,
which holds the ppn 6 PE loss constant and varies ONLY whether the helper is
named onto that spare core:

    D6 (dedicated core)  860.2 ms [847.1..879.9]   walk 0.374
    B6 (SMT siblings)    857.8 ms [829.5..899.4]   walk 0.365

NO DIFFERENCE.  2.4 ms apart on heavily overlapping ranges.  THE DEDICATED
CORE BUYS NOTHING over the SMT siblings the fix already derives by itself.

That is a clean null result and it is worth having: it means the explicit
FOF_HELPER_CPUS route -- and therefore the whole --core-spec=0 apparatus it
was invented to serve -- is unnecessary whenever an SMT sibling is free.
Reserve it for ppn 13/14, where the fix DECLINES because every sibling is
itself a PE, which is exactly the case it was originally written for.

====================================================================
4.  AN UNEXPECTED RESULT: ppn 6 MAY BEAT ppn 7 DESPITE 14% FEWER PEs
====================================================================
    S  (ppn 7, 7168 PEs)  914.1 ms [840.3..1000.3]   walk 0.426 [0.356..0.512]
    B6 (ppn 6, 6144 PEs)  857.8 ms [829.5.. 899.4]   walk 0.365 [0.350..0.373]

ppn 6 is 6.2% faster on the mean while running 14% fewer PEs, and its spread
is far tighter (walk range 0.023 s against 0.156 s).

I AM NOT CLAIMING IT.  The Iter0 ranges OVERLAP (840.3..899.4), n is 3-4, and
relay106 measured ppn 7 at 896.7 ms [894.8..898.5] in a different allocation,
between the two.  What IS solid is the tightness: the ppn 6 arms are far more
reproducible than the ppn 7 arm in the same job.

HYPOTHESIS, NOT MEASURED: this job runs +lci_ndevices 7 +backend_poll_thread 1,
so there are 7 poll threads per process.  At ppn 7 the PEs occupy all seven
cores and the poll threads must share; at ppn 6 one physical core is free for
them regardless of where the helper lands.  If that is right the benefit is
about the POLLERS, not the GPU helpers, and it would apply on CPU too.  That
is a testable claim -- a ppn 6 / ppn 7 pair on the CPU codepath would settle
it -- and it should not be repeated as fact until someone runs it.

====================================================================
5.  WHERE THE DAMAGE ACTUALLY LANDS -- MOSTLY NOT IN THE WALK
====================================================================
Kale's expectation was that the helper bit hardest during the treewalk.  The
walk does worsen: 0.426 -> 0.725 s, +0.299 s, +70%.  But Iteration 0 worsens
by +1557 ms, so THE WALK ACCOUNTS FOR ONLY ABOUT A FIFTH OF THE TOTAL DAMAGE.
phase1 barely moves (0.107 -> 0.113-0.116 s).

So something else in the iteration absorbs the remaining ~1.25 s, and this run
does not say what.  The obvious candidates are the union-find drain and the
phases the FOF3STAT time_s line already reports; I have NOT broken it down and
will not guess.  A per-phase comparison of one N rep against one S rep would
answer it directly from data already being printed.

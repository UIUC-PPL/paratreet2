PART A -- HIS TRACE, READ ON THE LOGIN NODE

 1. PROVENANCE FIRST.  Reading the .sts header was worth more than any of
    the analysis.  /lustre/orion/csc710/proj-shared/16node_gpu_proj_5307458,
    job 5307458: PROCESSORS 896, SMPMODE "7 128", COMMANDLINE "-i 1
    +pemap 1-7,9-15,... +lci_ndevices 4 +backend_poll_thread 2 +logsize
    20000000", CHARMVERSION v8.0.1-devel-175-g9af1de4b6, run 10.709 s.
 2. CONSEQUENCE.  The relay18 working hypothesis assumed the ppn-14 sweep
    shape and put the stalls in the QD-heavy uf2 bracket.  Both halves were
    wrong before a single record was parsed.
 3. THE RUN IS NOT PATHOLOGICAL.  busy 5029.9 s, idle 4280.6 s, OVERHEAD
    54.0 s = 0.58% of PE-time; per wall second the overhead never exceeds
    1.6%.
 4. THE GAPS.  326 OVERHEAD intervals >= 10 ms, 317 of them in the 10-60 ms
    band = 7.11 s = 0.076% of all PE-time, on 193 of 896 PEs.  A faithful
    match to the reported symptom -- a few PEs in overhead, everyone else
    idle -- and small.
 5. WHERE AND ON WHOM.  310 of 317 on POLLING threads (ranks 0,2,4,6 at
    poll 2), 44:1 over their co-tenants; the poller that owns its device
    alone (rank 6) carries about a third of what the sharing pollers do.
    Bounded by receive(ParticleMsg) 65.6% opening and 72.2% closing; 97.8%
    end on an arrival, 96.1% of those inter-process; resumption fan-in p50
    8 distinct peer processes, p90 19.  261 of 317 in the particle exchange
    at t = 3-7 s; 10 in the uf2 bracket.
 6. THE CONTROL THAT KILLED THE LOCK HYPOTHESIS -- see HEADLINE 3.  Stated
    caveat: the baseline is NOT a matched A/B (different paratreet version,
    CPU not GPU, -u serial not -u dist, ppn 14 not 7, 183 of 1792 PEs traced
    rather than all 896).  It controls the runtime's polling structure,
    which is what the hypothesis was about, and nothing more.
 7. TWO ANALYSIS ERRORS I MADE AND CAUGHT BEFORE REPORTING.  (a) Sorting
    events by (time, kind) inverts the same-microsecond END_IDLE/BEGIN_IDLE
    pairs reconverse emits and turns genuine idle into phantom overhead --
    this invented a "41x worse on his runtime" result that was pure
    artifact.  (b) Treating END_PACK/END_UNPACK as a return to overhead
    reclassified ordinary execution.  One validated state machine,
    scripts/relay18_state.py, hand-checked against a raw record dump,
    replaced both; every number in this relay comes from it.

11. AN ARTIFACT HAD TO BE REMOVED FIRST, AND IT WOULD HAVE INVERTED THE
    READING.  Every PE's trace opens with one OVERHEAD interval from its
    first record to its first BEGIN_IDLE -- trace startup, not a stall.  In
    five of six arms it lands inside the 10-60 ms band and contributes
    EXACTLY 896 intervals (one per PE, all at t = 0-1 s, all opened by
    "start of trace" and closed by "the PE went IDLE"), 62-85% of the raw
    band population.  It is absent from arm M rep 1 and from his own 5307458
    only because in those runs the input read was cold and that interval ran
    past 60 ms.  open_entry == -1 identifies it; every number below excludes
    it.

14. IT COSTS NOTHING MEASURABLE.  2.4-12.1 s of PE-time out of ~10,100 s,

    far more than a performance problem.


WHAT TO CHANGE

15. IF THE GOAL IS TO STOP SEEING THE SYMPTOM IN PROJECTIONS, run
    +lci_ndevices 1 +backend_poll_thread 7 at ppn 7.  It cuts the band
    population by 3.4x and confines it to one PE per process at no cost in
    wall time.  That is a COSMETIC fix and should be described as one.
16. DO NOT chase device counts for performance.  M, L and H are the same
    speed to within run-to-run noise.
17. THE REAL QUESTION FOR THE CHARM TEAM: why does the PE that calls
    progress() sit 10-60 ms inside the runtime without entering the idle
    state?  It happens on his branch and on main, at 1, 2 and 7 threads per
    domain, at the same per-poller rate.  That is reconverse scheduler
    behaviour, not an application question and not an LCI question.

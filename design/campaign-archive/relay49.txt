*** BUT IT IS TWO DEFECTS, AND HE ONLY KNEW ABOUT ONE.
    (1) charm configured CMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Debug with CMAKE_CXX_FLAGS_DEBUG=-g.
        That is not "error checking on" -- charm and reconverse are compiled
        with NO OPTIMIZATION AT ALL.  Worth 15.6%.
    (2) The application linked -tracemode projections.  Tracing was never
        ENABLED at runtime, but the hooks are compiled into the message path
        and phaseA is fine-grained.  Worth 7.7%.
    I found (2) only because a --with-production charm does not build
    libtrace-projections.a, so the production arm could not be built with
    PROJECTIONS=1 -- "charmc: No such tracemode projections".  Chasing that
    error revealed that the relay15 reference binary has TraceProjections 0
    while merged/FoF3.cpu has 461.  Had I run his three arms as specified,
    the answer would have read "the build flag explains two thirds" and sent
    somebody after a phantom remainder. ***

2.  THE PART OF HIS PREMISE THAT IS WRONG: THE 5.15 s REFERENCE DOES NOT
    REPRODUCE, ON ITS OWN BINARY, ON ITS OWN RUNTIME, IN ITS OWN CONFIG.
    relay15 (job 5304461) recorded Iter0 5144.8-5192 ms with phaseA
    1.966-2.000 s.  Arm A here is that exact binary (md5 2607c277...) on that
    exact runtime (~/software/charm/lib) with relay15's configuration
    (FOF_PE_SETS=14 MODE=1, STEALA, PB_PARTS, PHASEB_SLICE_MS, ppn 14,
    ndev 7 -- checked against relay15.txt item 4, it matches).  It gives
    7693.0 ms with phaseA 4.889 s.

    phaseA is 2.5x slower on a BYTE-IDENTICAL binary and runtime.  That is
    not the build, not the tree, not the configuration -- all three are held
    fixed.  So the "1.97 -> 4.76 s phaseA" signature in his brief is NOT
    evidence for the build hypothesis: phaseA is 4.89 s on the OLD build too,
    today.  The build hypothesis is carried entirely by the WALK
    (0.938 old vs 2.537 new-Debug vs 1.005 new-Release) and by uf2.

    REMAINING SUSPECTS for the 5.15 s gap, none tested: the runtime libfabric

    loaded instead of 2.3.1.

    UNTIL THEN, 5.15 s SHOULD NOT BE USED AS A BASELINE.  It is not

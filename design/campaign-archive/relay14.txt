WHY THIS RUN HAPPENED

 1. Relay13 left two candidates for what suppresses the stall in FoF.  In
    building the job to test them I read the source notes again and found
    the reframing: EVERY measurement of this bug -- Anvil jobs 19608513
    through 19661625 -- was made over MELLANOX INFINIBAND HDR
    (charm-notes/machines/anvil.md).  fof/FoF.C:13 opens the workaround
    comment with "On reconverse/LCI over InfiniBand".  The bug was
    localised BELOW the progress API, in the libfabric/IBV provider.
    Frontier is Slingshot/CXI through libfabric's cxi provider.  So the
    prior question is not which remedy holds the stall off here.  It is
    whether the bug is on this fabric at all.

 2. CORRECTION TO RELAY13 ITEM 16(b).  Job 19624352 ran the reproducer at
    BOTH 1 device/rank and 15 devices/rank: K=1 immune in both, K=8 at
    0.070%/0.210% over 10 ms, K=31 at 0.620%/0.430%, with the explicit
    conclusion "per-thread devices are not a workaround".  Device fan-out
    was never a live candidate.  My error was offering a hypothesis the
    notes had already killed; the fix is in place in both files.


THE HARNESS

 3. lci_multipeer.cpp DID NOT EXIST on Frontier -- it was written on Anvil
    and never copied.  Rebuilt from the description in
    charm_best_practices.md, matching it point by point:
      - PURE LCI: nm reports 0 converse/Cmi symbols; links the SAME
        liblci.so the production FoF binary links (LCI 8.0.1).
      - THE APP'S SHAPE: 128 ranks x 14 threads x 7 devices, with
        reconverse's exact mapping device_id = thread_id /
        ceil(nthreads/ndevices), so 2 threads share a device just as
        +ppn 14 +lci_ndevices 7 produces.
      - Rank 0 thread 0 pings round-robin over K distinct remote ranks,
        ONE message outstanding, 8-byte AMs; every other thread on every
        rank spins on lci::progress() for the whole run.
      - Silence before measuring, and NO barrier after it -- a barrier is
        itself traffic and would destroy the condition under test.
      - Each arm is a SEPARATE srun, so no arm warms the connections for
        the next one.  That is the variable under test.
    Two arms Anvil never ran: a heavy all-to-all traffic warm-up before
    the silence (relay13's warm-connections candidate), and a 100 ms
    keep-alive ring (the FOF_KEEPALIVE analogue, as workaround control).


THE RESULT

       K     mean us   p99 us   max us  <=8us    >1ms   >10ms   ANVIL >10ms
        1    6.55-6.58  34.8     78-294  94.6-95.1%  0    0      0.000%
        8    6.46-6.53  34.6     97-287  96.7%       0    0      0.070%
       16    6.67-6.77  33.0     80-260  93.6-95.6%  0    0      --

       64    7.30-7.51  34.8-35.7 80-84  89.1-90.5%  0    0      not run

    NOT ONE SAMPLE OVER 1 ms IN 160,000.  Worst round trip in the whole
    job: 580 us.  On Anvil, K=31 put 62 samples per 10,000 over TEN ms.

 5. HOW STRONG THE NULL IS.  0 events in 20,000 trials gives a 95% upper
    bound of 3/20,000 = 0.015%:
       K=8   Anvil 0.070%  ->  at least  4.7x below here
       K=31  Anvil 0.620%  ->  at least 41x   below here
    The healthy statistics differ too: Anvil's degraded K=16 control had
    an 89.1 us mean and its recovered state 24-27 us; Frontier's mean is
    6.5-7.5 us with 86-97% of samples in the 2-8 us mode at every K.
    There is no degraded regime here to recover from.

 6. LONG SILENCES (job 5303902).  Anvil's onset needed ~1 s and the sweep
    used Anvil's 5 s, so a slower-onset version on cxi would have been
    missed.  It does not exist:
       K=16 at 15/30/60 s: mean 6.94 / 6.96 / 6.93 us, 0 over 1 ms
       K=64 at 15/30/60 s: mean 7.39 / 7.41 / 7.41 us, 0 over 1 ms
    The mean is FLAT TO TWO DECIMALS across a 12x range of idle time.  The
    variable the entire trigger model rests on does nothing on this stack.
    Running total across both jobs: 220,000 round trips, 0 over 1 ms.

 7. THE PEER-COUNT AXIS DOES EXIST HERE, and it is tiny: mean 6.58 us at
    K=1 rising monotonically to 7.51 us at K=64, fast-mode fraction 95.1%
    falling to 89.1%.  That is +14% of mean across a 64x peer count,
    entirely inside the microsecond band.  On Anvil the same axis moved
    the mean 3.5x and pushed 0.6% of samples past 10 ms.  So the harness
    is sensitive to the right variable; the fabric just does not break.

 8. THE TWO NEW ARMS.
    (a) WARM CONNECTIONS is not the mechanism, and the question has
        dissolved.  Heavy traffic before the silence makes the mean WORSE
        (8.5-10.5 us against 6.7-7.5 us at the same K; max 520-580 us
        against 80-280) -- residual traffic still draining.  Since neither
        arm stalls there is nothing for it to suppress.
    (b) THE RING DOES SOMETHING MEASURABLE, AND IT IS NOT WHAT IT WAS
        BUILT FOR.  With the 100 ms ring on, p99 falls from 32.95/34.63 us
        to 23.09/23.27 us, consistently in both reps -- about 12 us off
        the tail.  The mean does not move (6.62 vs 6.67).  Twelve
        microseconds of p99 on a microsecond-scale operation is not the
        removal of 10-25 ms stalls.


WHAT THIS SETTLES, AND WHAT I RECOMMEND

 9. The relay13 question is answered, outside both candidates: neither the
    keep-alive ring nor the polling configuration is holding the stall off
    on Frontier.  There is no stall on Frontier.  The credit belongs to
    the fabric and provider -- Slingshot/CXI, libfabric, LCI 8.0.1 -- and
    the bug is an InfiniBand-path behaviour this machine's path does not
    have.

10. THE RING IS DEAD WEIGHT ON FRONTIER.  160 msg/s job-wide, invisible in
    FoF's timings (relay13: -12 ms, inside noise), and 12 us of p99 in a
    microbenchmark.  KEEP IT anyway: it is free, and it is load-bearing on
    Anvil, where jobs 19624352/19661625 are not in question.  What should
    change is the DESCRIPTION, not the code.

11. ONE COMMENT IS WORTH EDITING, and I have not edited it because no code
    change was asked for.  fof/FoF.C:13-33 presents the ring as the fix
    for FoF's uf2-bracket stalls without naming the fabric it was measured
    on.  One sentence -- InfiniBand-path behaviour, not reproducible on
    Slingshot/CXI as of 2026-08-18, jobs 5303887/5303902 -- would stop the
    next person inheriting the wrong model.  Say the word and it is a
    three-line patch.

12. BUILDS.md IS STALE ON ONE POINT.  It records the charm build against
    libfabric 1.20.1; the stack now resolves 2.3.1 at run time
    (/opt/cray/libfabric/2.3.1/lib64/libfabric.so.1), with 1.15.0.0,
    1.15.2.0 and 1.20.1 also installed.  Both the FoF binary and this
    reproducer load 2.3.1.  Nothing in this relay depends on it, but a
    provider-level behaviour is exactly what a provider upgrade changes.


CAVEATS

13. THIS IS A REWRITTEN HARNESS, not the Anvil binary, so a null could in
    principle be a harness that fails to exercise the bug.  Three things
    argue it is faithful: it matches the documented structure point by
    point; it reproduces the healthy behaviour the notes describe (a
    2-8 us mode holding 86-97% of samples); and it shows the same
    qualitative K dependence, three orders of magnitude smaller.  WHAT
    WOULD SETTLE IT BEYOND ARGUMENT: run this same file on Anvil and
    recover the 0.62% at K=31.  One small job, on a machine I do not have
    in this session.

14. Scale differs from Anvil (32 ranks x 15 threads there; 128 x 14 x 7
    here, chosen to mirror FoF).  Anvil stalled at both 1 and 15 devices
    per rank, so 7 is not a special case, and 128 ranks is a LARGER
    fan-out than the 32 that stalled.

15. This says nothing about Anvil.  The ring remains load-bearing there.

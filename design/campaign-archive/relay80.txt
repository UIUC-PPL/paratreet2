1.  THE LANDED CODE IS EXACT -- the question Kale asked
====================================================================

What landed is the relay79 patch pair PLUS local_union's size merges gated
under the same knob and a prune_components abort under FOF_UF_SIZES=0.  The
local_union gating is a real code change relative to what relay79 measured,
and it is semantically inert for FoF3 only if nothing reads sizes.

    relay80 cpu-szOFF   EXACT     relay82 cpu-szOFF x3   EXACT
    relay80 gpu-szOFF x2 EXACT    relay81 both szOFF arms EXACT

Seven independent sizes-off arms, CPU and GPU, production and traced, all
EXACT on components AND max_size.  The gating is inert, confirmed.

*** AND THE LANDED FORM IS BETTER THAN MINE.  My relay79 caveat was that
    local_union still did a direct "size +=", so under the knob the field was
    INCONSISTENT rather than absent.  Gating it makes the field uniformly
    unmaintained, which is what makes the prune_components abort a complete
    guard rather than a partial one. ***

3.  THE GPU ARM -- larger than CPU, and my prediction was wrong
====================================================================

    gpu-szON   2630.9 ms  [2630.9..2630.9]   walk 0.574 s
    gpu-szOFF  2562.2 ms  [2547.9..2576.5]   walk 0.503 s
    -68.7 ms   -2.61%     ranges SEPARATED, 2 reps each, both EXACT

I predicted in the job header that the GPU effect might be SMALLER, because
FOF_GPU_PHASE1 forces peSets=1 (src/common.h) so the GPU arm lacks the ~794k
same-process unions the PE-set split manufactures on CPU.  THE PREMISE WAS
WRONG, and the census says why: fb2_UNION is ~427k on GPU against ~437k on
CPU, nearly the same.  Those 794k same-process unions never used add_size
MESSAGES at all -- local_union handles them inline -- so removing add_size was
always about the DISTRIBUTED unions, and those counts barely differ.

====================================================================
4.  DID THE DRAIN SHRINK, OR ONLY THE QUEUING?  -- the traces
====================================================================

The open question from relay79.  add_size is fire-and-forget off the find
dependence chain, so the -2% could have been queuing relief alone.

    arm         drain    uf protocol CPU in it   util    active PEs
    cpu-szON    311 ms        1015.8 PE-ms       0.62%      34.5
    cpu-szOFF   255 ms         769.0 PE-ms       0.63%      33.6
    gpu-szON    248 ms         626.3 PE-ms       0.65%      28.5
    gpu-szOFF   181 ms         399.2 PE-ms       0.68%      33.3

    CPU drain  -56 ms  (-18%)      GPU drain  -67 ms  (-27%)

*** THE DRAIN ITSELF SHRANK.  And that is not an artefact of how the drain is
    defined.  The drain is the span of union-find activity, and add_size
    executions ARE union-find activity, so deleting them could shorten the
    measured window by construction.  scripts/relay81-findonly.py re-measures
    with add_size EXCLUDED from the group, i.e. the FIND cascade only --
    and the drain lengths come out IDENTICAL: 311/255 on CPU, 248/181 on GPU.
    add_size never extended past the find cascade.  The find cascade genuinely
    finishes earlier. ***

WHERE THE WALL SAVING COMES FROM, then:
    GPU  -68.7 ms wall,  -67 ms of it is the drain     -- essentially all
    CPU  -110.5 ms wall, -56 ms of it is the drain     -- about half, the
         rest from the walk-concurrent portion of the cascade

*** A LIMIT ON THE CPU-IN-DRAIN COLUMN, stated so nobody over-reads it.  A
    same-chare add_size is a DIRECT CALL inlined into find_boss2, which runs
    inside insertDataFindBoss's entry-method execution -- so local add_size
    work is charged to insertDataFindBoss and is still inside the "find only"
    numbers.  The DRAIN LENGTHS are clean; the PE-ms columns are not a pure
    find-versus-add_size split and should not be quoted as one. ***

Whole-run union protocol CPU falls too: CPU 4429.8 -> 3388.0 PE-ms (-23.5%),
GPU 3960.9 -> 2812.2 (-29.0%).  Walk EP CPU is unchanged in both arms
(22247.8 vs 22285.4 on CPU), so the walk BRACKET shortens because the
concurrent cascade finishes sooner, not because the walk itself does less.

dist -- before charging the node stage itself.  So the hierarchy closes most of
the remaining gap and still does not open one.  NOT MEASURED.

Read that as the headroom map.  The current rule is exactly the measured

m2 is the natural candidate to beat piece degree because it is the same

5.  WHERE THIS LEAVES KALE'S PROPOSAL
====================================================================

  - The within-process walk phase is NOT indicated.  A ~ B (4.00x vs 3.89x)
    says the local-local pairs are not where the skew is, so pooling and
    balancing them cannot reach it -- and the phase would additionally give up
    the latency hiding it currently provides by filling fetch waits, which
    section 2 shows is where most of the PE time actually goes.
  - The m2-weighted static tie-break is the indicated next step, per Kale's own
    rule, and it is one dump-and-score away rather than a build.
  - The straggler PEs are stable across arms and across jobs, so a STATIC
    remedy is the right shape -- the skew is not something that moves run to
    run and needs dynamic balancing.

NOT MEASURED: any scale other than 16 nodes; the GPU arm; the node-level stage
of section 3; and m2 itself.

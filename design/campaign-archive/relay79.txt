THE CLAIM UNDER TEST (relay78 section 10, from the source): the union-find
`size` field is never read in FoF3.  The app seeds it, the library maintains it
through add_size, set_component propagates it -- and then
collectComponentLabels returns componentNumber ONLY.  Sizes and max_size come
from the LABEL histogram at the end.

2.  THE KNOB GATE AND THE INTERNAL CONTROL
====================================================================

The knob is positive on both sides, and one identity is exact:

    arm A   addsize_root 437,962-439,761   addsize_SKIPPED 0
    arm B   addsize_root 0                 addsize_SKIPPED 436,538 / 438,207
    and in BOTH B reps  addsize_SKIPPED == fb2_UNION  EXACTLY
      (435,751 == 435,751 walk;  437,563 == 437,563 walk)

That identity is the check that the gating is in the right place: one skip per
union, no more and no fewer.

INTERNAL CONTROL -- skipping add_size must not change the union protocol
itself, and it does not.  Across the four arms:

    counter                 A reps              B reps
    fb1_root            544,878 / 543,359   541,184 / 543,258

====================================================================
3.  WHAT WAS REMOVED
====================================================================

    add_size calls per run        ~479,000   (438,000 root + 41,000 forwarding)
    of the drain's protocol messages   30.4%
    cost measured                  -88.9 ms of a 4630 ms iteration

The prediction in relay78 section 10 was hedged: add_size is NOT on the find
dependence chain -- it is fire-and-forget, issued just before the parent write,
and nothing waits on it -- so removing a side flow need not shorten a
latency-bound drain.  It shortened it anyway, and the -79 ms in the WALK
bracket says why: Charm entry methods are non-preemptive, so every add_size
execution was delaying whatever find message sat behind it in the queue.
Removing the traffic removes that queuing delay.

====================================================================
4.  WHAT THIS IS AND IS NOT
====================================================================

IT IS: the cheapest result of the campaign.  It removes work rather than adding
a mechanism, it needs no trigger -- the thing that killed the wave -- and it is
one env knob.

IT IS NOT a library change that can be pushed as-is.  unionFindLib is shared,
and a client that DOES read sizes (dense mode, prune_components) would break
silently.  The knob form is right; what belongs upstream is a documented
no-sizes mode, defaulting to the current behaviour, with FoF3 opting in.  Note
also that local_union still does its direct "size +=" (unionFindLib.C:656-659);
those are local and message-free, so they were left alone, and it means the
field is inconsistent rather than absent under the knob -- which is exactly why
this must be opt-in rather than a deletion.

NOT MEASURED: the GPU arm, and a traced run of arm B to confirm the drain
itself shrank rather than only the queuing around it.  The wall and the walk
bracket both moved, so the direction is not in doubt; the mechanism inside the
drain is inferred, not seen.

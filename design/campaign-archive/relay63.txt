    arm                     ndev/poll  ntasks  mean       range   vs A

    D  p7-phys   leaf 128     7 / 1      128   7057.3      53.7    --
    E  p14-smt   leaf 128     7 / 2      128   7444.7      50.4   +5.49%
    F  p7-phys   leaf  32     4 / 2      128   4794.4      11.8   -0.35%

WHAT HAPPENED TO THE ppn RESULT, in three steps:

    relay48, Debug build, leaf 128   p7 7813-7916  p14-smt 9550.6   +22%
    relay64 D/E, production, leaf 128  7057.3              7444.7   +5.5%
    relay64 A/B, production, leaf  32  4811.3              4850.8   +0.8%

The Debug build was inflating the ppn-14 penalty by a factor of four, and
the wrong leaf size was inflating what remained by a factor of seven.  What
survives is 0.82%, with non-overlapping ranges (A [4791.9, 4825.5] against
B [4833.9, 4861.4]), so it is a real effect and it is nearly worthless.

AND relay48's SMT DECOMPOSITION INVERTS AT leaf 32.  relay48 had p14-phys
at +2.6% and p14-smt at +22%, i.e. putting the extra 896 PEs on SMT
siblings was the expensive part and 4-processes-of-14 on physical cores was
nearly free.  At leaf 32 it is the other way round: p14-smt +0.82%,
p14-phys +3.15%.  The 1792-PE SMT shape is now the BETTER of the two ppn-14
shapes.  I am not going to explain that from this job; it is one more
result that was measured at the wrong leaf size and needs re-deriving if
anyone wants the mechanism.

F IS THE BRIDGE TO relay62 AND IT HOLDS.  relay62 measured ppn 7 at leaf 32
with ndev 4 / poll 2 and got 4811.9 ms; F is that exact configuration in
this job and gives 4794.4 ms, 17.5 ms apart across two allocations.  A vs F
is 16.9 ms with overlapping ranges, so the product-rule choice (ndev 7 /
poll 1 against ndev 4 / poll 2) does not matter at ppn 7.  The two jobs can
be read together.

4.  WHAT THE CPU-ONLY RECOMMENDATION IS NOW
====================================================================

    ppn 7, PEMAP7 (physical cores only), -l 32, production build.
    4785-4811 ms.

Against what stood this morning (7466.3 ms, ppn 14, leaf 128, production)
that is -35.9%.  Against the Debug-era ppn-7 number in RESUME (7892.2 ms)
it is -39.4%.

The ppn part of that recommendation is now carried by 0.8%, not 17%.  If
there is any reason to prefer ppn 14 -- and on this evidence there is
nearly no cost to it -- that choice is now open in a way it was not.

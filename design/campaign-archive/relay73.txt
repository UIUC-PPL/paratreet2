4.  A DEFECT OF MINE, RECORDED
====================================================================

The first submission of this job (5324674) died after 50 s with
"line 70: label: unbound variable" and measured nothing.  Cause:

    local label=$1 bin=$2 log=$OUT/${label}.log      # WRONG

bash expands EVERY argument to `local` before performing ANY of the
assignments, so ${label} is still unset when $OUT/${label}.log is expanded,
and `set -u` aborts.  Every earlier job script in this campaign split that
across two `local` statements, which is why none of them hit it.  Fixed,
the idiom verified in isolation, and a comment left in the script saying
why it must stay split.  No measurement was affected -- only a queue wait.

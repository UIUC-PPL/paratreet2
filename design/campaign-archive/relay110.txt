ONE COUNTER MOVES: suppression, -5.25%.  It is 2.8% of prunes and it moves

====================================================================
2.  THE SUPPORTING NUMBERS, AND THE CPU CONTRAST
====================================================================
  capping U -> 128     pool_MB    placeholders   requests   canopy_fills   walk
  GPU (relay106)        -51.8%       -53.9%        -0.8%      +343,844    -16.4%
  CPU (relay97)         -29.6%       -30.0%        -0.7%      +347,600    +31.1%

REQUESTS ARE FLAT ON BOTH PATHS.  Misses do not change when capping, on GPU or
CPU.  That removes the refill-latency explanation from the other side as well:
there is no extra miss traffic to be latent about.

Placeholders are 85.0% of used nodes uncapped and 81.3% capped (GPU).  The
uncapped pack costs ~77 MB more pool per process and 291 M more placeholder
nodes across the job, allocated interleaved with content.

Uncapped requests: GPU 6,168,760 against CPU 12,109,437, a ratio of 0.51 --
consistent in direction with the GPU walk being thinner (487k edges against
CPU's 1.24M, leaf 128), though a factor of two is a moderate difference, not
an order of magnitude.  I am not going to call that "far below".

THE CPU CONTRAST IS THE PART I CANNOT CLOSE.  The CPU gets the same KIND of
pool relief (-29.6%) and the same flat requests, yet its walk gets 31% WORSE.
The only quantity that rises on both paths is canopy_fills, and it rises by
nearly the same ABSOLUTE amount (347,600 CPU, 343,844 GPU).  So whatever the
CPU pays for those fills, the GPU does not -- or the GPU's larger dilution
relief (-51.8% against -29.6%) simply outweighs it.  This report does not
decide between those, and neither does any measurement currently on disk.
CAVEAT: the CPU +31.1% rests on relay97's uncapped arm at n=1.  The reproducible
CPU walk penalty is relay96's 64-node sweep, n=3, 0.409 -> 0.547 s.

====================================================================
3.  WHAT IS ESTABLISHED, AND WHAT IS STILL INFERENCE
====================================================================
ESTABLISHED: the GPU walk does the same work when capped and takes 16.4% less
time.  Whatever the mechanism, it is NOT that capping changes what the walk
does.  Any explanation resting on extra fetches, extra misses, or extra
traversal is dead.

STILL INFERENCE: that the remaining cause is cache/TLB locality from a smaller,
less diluted pool.  It is the reading that fits, it made a prediction that
held, and no competing reading has been offered -- but "same work, less time"
is consistent with several memory-side stories and this evidence cannot
separate them.  It should be written as a well-supported reading, not as a
measured mechanism.

             in the module's spack tree.  Fixable by passing
             -DCMAKE_C_FLAGS="-I$PAPI_DIR/include" and matching link flags.

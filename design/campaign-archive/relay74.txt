6.  RECOMMENDATION
====================================================================

1. THE SORT+SCAN PAIR PATH IS THE WIN.  It is worth -283.6 ms on the CPU
   iteration (-5.9% of arm B) and it is the whole of the -6.4% relay72
   reported.  Keep it.
2. THE PREFIX REMOVAL DOES NOT PAY FOR ITSELF ON THESE ARMS.  Wall-neutral,
   and slightly negative on the histogram row.  The brief always said its
   payoff was the compression wave it enables rather than these arms -- but
   the wave does not pay either (item 3), so on current evidence the prefix
   removal has no measured benefit at 2B.  It is not harmful and it is a
   prerequisite for other work; I am not recommending reverting it, only
   that it stop being credited with the sort path's gain.
3. DO NOT FLIP THE WAVE DEFAULT.  Mode 1 costs 1.1%, mode 2 costs 12.7%,
   and the wave rewrites ~256 parents at 2B.  Both modes are correct, so
   the knob can stay for further work at other scales.

   150-300 ms near-idle drain it targets still exists at 2B.  If it does
   not, the wave has nothing to win here and the result is explained; if it
   does, the wave is firing at the wrong point rather than being a bad idea.

relay48 -- THE ppn-14 PENALTY IS SMT NOT PAYING FOR ITSELF.  IT IS NOT OS
INTERFERENCE.  Jobs 5320076 and 5320090, 16 nodes, 2B, CPU-only, 3 reps per
arm.  Plus the traces for the best GPU shape, job 5320106.  2026-08-21.

*** THE FOUR PLACEMENTS.  1792 PEs cannot be run without SMT -- there are only
    896 physical cores on 16 nodes -- but 896 PEs CAN be run with it, and
    holding PE count at 896 in three arms is what finally separates the
    variables.

      placement              PEs  cores  SMT      mean        vs p7-phys
      p7-phys   8proc x 7PE   896    56   no    7813-7916 ms   baseline
      p14-phys  4proc x14PE   896    56   no       8130.5 ms     +2.6%
      p14-smt   8proc x14PE  1792    56   yes      9550.6 ms      +22%
      p7-smt    8proc x 7PE   896    28   yes     13963.0 ms      +79%

    All arms EXACT. ***

*** SMT IS WORTH ABOUT 12% OF THROUGHPUT ON THIS WORKLOAD.  p7-smt runs the
    SAME 896 PEs on HALF the physical cores and is 1.787x slower.  If the
    second hardware thread gave nothing, halving the cores would cost exactly
    2x.  It cost 1.787x, so SMT recovers 2/1.787 = 1.12x. ***

*** WHICH SETTLES IT: at ppn 14, going from 896 PEs on physical cores to 1792
    PEs using SMT -- same ppn, same 56 physical cores -- is 8130.5 -> 9550.6
    ms, +17.5%.  ADDING PARALLELISM ON SMT THREADS IS A NET LOSS HERE.  The
    extra 896 PEs buy ~12% more compute capacity and cost more than that in
    parallel overhead. ***

1.  KALE'S OS-INTERFERENCE HYPOTHESIS: MEASURED, AND IT IS NO.
    He asked whether the ppn-14 phase-3 cost could be the same 10-16 ms
    preemptions we chased on the GPU arm, on the reasoning that ppn 14 leaves
    nowhere for OS work to land.  The kernel's own cumulative accounting --
    /proc/<tid>/schedstat run_delay, summed over the PE threads on the batch
    node, differenced across each run:

      arm         PE cpu_ms      runqueue wait    as % of PE cpu
      p14-smt     ~2,070,000     2143-2376 ms      0.10-0.12%
      p14-phys      ~820,000      100-148 ms       0.01-0.02%
      p7-phys       ~830,000      0.1-530 ms       0.00-0.06%
      p7-smt      ~1,350,000      484-1907 ms      0.04-0.14%

    At ppn 14 the PEs wait 0.11% of their CPU time on the runqueue, about
    19 ms per PE over a 9.5 s run.  That cannot produce a 17-22% penalty.
    And p14-phys, which fills every physical core and leaves the least room of
    all, has the LOWEST wait in the set.

    THE COMPARISON THAT MAKES THIS CONCLUSIVE: on the GPU arm the victim PE
    showed 2.7 SECONDS of runqueue wait against 0.0 ms for unshared PEs.  The
    signal is unmistakable when it is real.  It is not here.  The reasoning
    was sound; the machine is not paying it.

2.  WHAT EACH PAIR ISOLATES, stated so nobody over-reads a single comparison.
      p7-phys vs p14-phys  same PEs, same cores, no SMT either.  Differs in
                           PEs-per-process (7 vs 14) and process count
                           (128 vs 64).  +2.6%.  Fat processes cost little.
      p7-phys vs p7-smt    same PEs, same ppn, same process count.  Differs in
                           physical cores (56 vs 28) with SMT engaged.  The
                           SMT throughput number comes from here.
      p14-phys vs p14-smt  same ppn, same physical cores.  Differs in PE count
                           (896 vs 1792), SMT, and process count.  +17.5%.

    CONFOUND I FLAGGED BEFORE RUNNING AND AM RESTATING: engaging SMT at fixed
    PE count necessarily halves the physical cores in use, so p7-smt also has
    half the aggregate L3 and memory bandwidth.  Both effects push the same
    way.  The 12% figure is therefore the COMBINED value of the second
    hardware thread, which is the number the launch-shape decision needs; it
    is not a clean measure of SMT execution-unit sharing alone.

3.  AN ARM FAILED FIRST TIME AND IT WAS MY ERROR, not the application's.
    p14-phys aborted in all three reps of job 5320076 with
      Error: CmiSetCPUAffinity failed to bind PE #53 to PU P#61
    An srun step launched with --ntasks=64 INHERITS --cpus-per-task=14 from
    the batch header, so the step received only 4 x 14 = 56 logical CPUs per
    node and the later processes' pemap entries (cores 33-63) were outside
    the step's cpuset.  Four tasks per node covering 56 physical cores needs
    --cpus-per-task=28 on the srun.  Job 5320090 re-ran it correctly.
    WORTH KEEPING: a step that changes --ntasks must revisit --cpus-per-task,
    because the inherited value silently shrinks the step's cpuset rather
    than failing.

4.  WHERE THIS LEAVES THE SHAPE QUESTION.
    CPU-only, current defaults, 2B on 16 nodes: ppn 7 is the right launch
    shape, and the reason is now known -- SMT returns ~12% while the extra
    896 PEs cost more than that.  It is NOT the AUTO PE-set default (relay47),
    NOT OS interference (this report), and only 2.6 points of it are
    PEs-per-process.
    Together with the GPU arm, both converge on ppn 7 for different reasons:
    on GPU the SMT siblings are free headroom for ROCm's helper threads; on
    CPU the SMT siblings do not pay for the parallelism they add.

====================================================================
3.  WHY THE MECHANISM CANNOT WORK, IN ONE PARAGRAPH
====================================================================

A wave pass costs O(all vertices) in sweep plus a global message cascade that
is itself latency-bound in exactly the way the drain is.  What it can win is
capped at ~260 parent rewrites, and pass 2 already has all of them.  Fixed
large cost, fixed tiny benefit, so firing more often can only lose -- which is
precisely what B and C measure, and why halving the period doubles the loss.

This is the constraint the relay75 addendum named this morning.  The addendum
retracted "the forest must be deep and STABLE" and said the real constraint is
COST PER FIRING, because compression degrades gracefully and stability is not
required.  relay77 confirms that reading from the other side: stability was
never the problem, and firing during the cascade -- exactly what relay75
section 6 point 3 asked for -- makes things much worse, not better.

The wave has now been tested at THREE trigger points, and the evidence is
complete:
    at the barrier, -E 16   forest already settled     ~259 rewrites, +57 ms
    at the barrier, -E  0   forest not yet built       ~259 rewrites, +29 ms
    periodic, mid-cascade   the forest is deep here     255-263 rewrites, +71%
The rewrite count is the same in all three.  There is no trigger point that
helps, because the trigger was never what limited it.

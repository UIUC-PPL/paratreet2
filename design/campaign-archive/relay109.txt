3.  WHAT THE SHAPE MEANS
====================================================================
EVERY phase is hit.  The damage is not concentrated anywhere; it is a tax on
the whole iteration.

AND THE RELATIVE ORDERING IS INVERTED FROM THE ABSOLUTE ONE.  The short
phases suffer worst as multiples -- edge_gather 21.6x, uf2 9.1x, loadCache
8.7x -- while phase3_walk, by far the longest phase, is only 1.5x.

That is the signature of a FIXED-SIZE PREEMPTION rather than a proportional
slowdown: a helper stealing ~16 ms slices costs a 5 ms phase everything and a
460 ms phase relatively little.  It matches the 16.006 ms kernel-preemption
slice the campaign already measured in the QD-settle work, and it is why the
fix is worth so much more at 128 nodes than the 16-node -22.8% suggested --
at scale there are more short phases and more synchronisation points for a
fixed stall to land on.

RECONCILING THIS WITH THE EXPECTATION.  Kale's expectation was that the helper
bit hardest during the treewalk.  In ABSOLUTE terms the walk is indeed the
single largest contributor at 20.9% of the damage -- but only because it is
the longest phase.  Per unit of time spent in it, the walk is the phase that
suffers LEAST.  Both readings are true and they are not in conflict; the
report says so because "it bites the treewalk" would be a misleading summary
of this table.

4.  A BUG OF MINE THAT MADE THIS RERUN NECESSARY
====================================================================
relay108 passed "r$r" as the log label for all four arms of a rep, so all four
wrote to the SAME $OUT/r$r.log and only the last arm of each rep survived.
The .dat files were keyed by arm, which is why relay108's SUMMARY is intact --
but per-phase data existed for one N rep, two B6 reps, and NO S rep.  The
comparison could not be made from disk.  relay109 puts the arm in the label
and also appends every time_s line to phases-N.txt / phases-S.txt.

A SECOND ONE, SMALLER: the per-phase table was supposed to print inside the
job.  My edit to the summary block did not match the target text and I did not
verify it before submitting, so the job printed relay108's old summary legend
instead.  The table above was computed afterwards from the phases-*.txt files
the run did write correctly.  Nothing was lost, but the job did not do what
its own script said it would.

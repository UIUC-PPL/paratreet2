5.  WHAT I THINK THE NUMBERS MEAN, and what I do not claim.

    THE FIXED ARM IS PRESERVED.  3504.9 against 3635.2 ms, 130 ms apart, well
    inside the spread.  That is the question the test was for, and the answer
    is yes.

    THE UNFIXED ARM MOVED MORE (+465 ms), and that is what makes the ratio
    look better.  I would NOT report "the all-latest stack improves the fix to
    -32%".  Two readings fit and one rep cannot separate them: either the new
    runtime costs the unfixed configuration something, or the unfixed
    configuration is simply noisier.  The second is plausible on mechanism --
    the thing the fix removes is a stochastic preemption, so removing it
    should reduce variance as well as the mean -- and the data is weakly
    consistent with it: across the two jobs the FIXED ppn 7 arm moved 130 ms
    while the UNFIXED one moved 465 ms.  Two samples is not evidence.  If this
    matters, three reps per arm would settle it.

    THE ppn 14 SPREAD IS WIDER THAN I SAID.  C and D differ ONLY by an
    environment variable the fix ignored (it declined and said so), yet they
    are 666 ms apart here, against 403 ms in job 5319480 and 394 ms in
    5319180.  So the single-rep spread at ppn 14 is 400-670 ms on ~5.5-6 s,
    7-11%.  My earlier "~400 ms" was the low end of three samples.  Nothing in
    this report or relay43 turns on a delta smaller than that.
